Home Security Systems Explained: CCTV, Access Control & Alarms

Sedra Electric · 10 August 2026 · 5 min read

Real security isn't a single camera — it's several systems working together to deter, detect and alert. This guide breaks down the four pillars of a modern security setup and how to choose what's right for your home or business.

1. CCTV & surveillance

Cameras are your eyes on the property. Modern IP cameras deliver sharp footage day and night, record continuously, and let you watch live from your phone anywhere. Good camera placement — entrances, perimeter, blind spots — matters more than sheer camera count.

2. Access control

Access control decides who can enter and when — via cards, PIN codes, fingerprints or your phone. It replaces easy-to-copy keys, logs every entry, and lets you grant or revoke access instantly. Essential for offices, gated homes and shared buildings.

3. Intrusion alarm

Door/window sensors and motion detectors trigger an alarm and send you an instant alert the moment someone tries to break in — turning a passive system into an active deterrent.

4. Fire & safety detection

Smoke and fire detection protects lives, not just property. Integrated with the rest of the system, an alarm can also unlock doors and notify the right people automatically.

The real advantage comes from integration: when cameras, access control, alarms and fire detection speak to each other and to your phone, you get one clear picture instead of four disconnected apps.

How to choose the right setup

  • Map your risk points first — entrances, perimeter, valuables.
  • Prioritise camera placement and quality over quantity.
  • Choose systems that integrate and are remotely accessible.
  • Confirm recording storage, warranty and local support.
