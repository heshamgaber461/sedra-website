10 Smart Home Scenes That Will Change How You Live

Sedra Electric · 10 August 2026 · 6 min read

The real magic of a smart home isn't controlling one device — it's scenes: one tap that sets lights, climate, shades, audio and security exactly how you want them. Here are 10 practical scenes that make daily life easier, safer and more efficient.

1. Good Morning

Gently raises the shades, sets a comfortable temperature, and eases the lights on — a calm start to the day, automatically at your wake-up time.

2. Leaving Home

One tap at the door: all lights off, AC to eco mode, shades closed, and the security system armed. No more wondering if you left something on.

3. Welcome Home

As you arrive, the entrance and living areas light up, the AC returns to comfort, and your favourite music starts — a warm welcome every time.

4. Movie Night

Dims the lights, closes the shades, and powers on the TV or projector and sound — cinema mode without touching five remotes.

5. Good Night

Turns off all lights, locks the doors, arms the alarm, and sets the bedroom to your ideal sleeping temperature.

6. Dinner / Entertaining

Sets warm dining lighting, background music at the right level, and comfortable climate for guests — one tap before they arrive.

7. Away / Vacation

Simulates presence by turning lights on and off naturally while you're away, keeping the home looking lived-in and secure.

8. Energy Saver

Optimises AC and lighting across the home to cut consumption — especially powerful when paired with solar.

9. Someone at the Door

The intercom shows who's there on your phone or panel, and you can talk, unlock or ignore — from anywhere.

10. All Off

A single "everything off" that switches off lights, media and non-essential loads — perfect for leaving in a hurry.

Scenes are what make a smart home feel effortless. The best ones are tailored to how you actually live — which is exactly what a good integrator designs with you.
